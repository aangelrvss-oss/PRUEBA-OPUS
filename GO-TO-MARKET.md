# GO-TO-MARKET.md

Timing drives everything: the 182 is filed 1–31 January. Today is 7 October
2026, which leaves about 12 weeks before the first season. Searches for
"modelo 182" peak in January. Being found in December and January matters more
than anything done in summer.

## Before the first user (October)

1. **Validate the file with AEAT** (VALIDATION T3) through one friendly entity
   that has a certificate. Nothing goes out before this passes.
2. Publish on a stable domain as a static site (BUILD.md). The artifact link is
   private and is for review only.

## First 10 users (November – mid-December)

Direct, personal, and where treasurers already ask for help:

- **Federations and NGO platforms with member services:** regional
  platforms of the third sector (e.g. Plataforma del Voluntariado, regional
  coordinators of NGOs) and the Asociación Española de Fundaciones, which
  publishes a yearly 182 reminder and a tax guide on donations. Offer a free
  webinar: "El 182 de 2026: qué cambió en la recurrencia y cómo calcularla en 10
  minutos", using the demo.
- **Volunteer-centre newsletters** (municipal "centros de voluntariado", social
  action offices of universities): a short piece on the 2025 recurrence change,
  with the tool as the practical takeaway.
- **Personal outreach to 30 small foundations** listed in public registries
  (protectorate registries publish names and contact data of foundations).
  Message: the rule change, a two-line example of a donor who stops being
  recurrent, and the link. Ask for a 15-minute call, which is also the
  validation interview.
- **Two or three gestorías that specialise in nonprofits.** Offer the
  multi-entity plan free for the first season in exchange for feedback.

Success criterion: 10 entities generate their real file and import it without
errors in January 2027.

## First 100 users (December – January, then next season)

- **Search, for the January peak:** one useful page per question people type:
  "modelo 182 recurrencia 2025", "modelo 182 excel", "cómo importar fichero
  modelo 182", "porcentaje de deducción modelo 182 personas físicas". Each page
  answers the question with a worked example and links to the tool. These pages
  must exist by early December to be indexed.
- **Weekly worked examples in December** in third-sector groups (LinkedIn groups
  of fundraisers and NGO administrators, AEFr fundraising community): one real
  case per week (a monthly member, a company sponsor, an anonymous cash donation)
  with how the 182 treats it.
- **Referral inside the product:** the downloaded history CSV is next year's
  input. Each user who returns brings a peer when asked "¿conoces a otra
  entidad que lo haga a mano?" at download time (once a backend exists).
- **Templates for popular tools:** column presets for the exports of common
  membership tools and banks (Bizum/transfer exports), so "export from X, drop
  into Cuadra" works without mapping.

## What I will not do

- Paid ads before the AEAT import is validated and the message is tested.
- Generic social media posting without a specific audience.
